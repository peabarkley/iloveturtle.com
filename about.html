About iLoveTurtle

The people, the process, and why you can trust what you read here.

Our Mission

iLoveTurtle exists for one reason: to make reliable, science-based turtle care information easy to find. The internet is full of conflicting advice, old forum posts, and unverified claims. Our job is to cut through that and publish guides a keeper can actually act on — safely.

Who We Are

We are a small independent team of long-term turtle and tortoise keepers, science writers, and consulting veterinarians. We are not a pet store, and we are not paid by any brand to recommend products. That independence is what allows us to stay honest.

cc — Lead Writer

cc has kept reptiles for over a decade and specializes in translating herpetology research into practical care advice. She writes most of the anatomy and species content on this site. She is the primary author behind our "Do Turtles Have Vocal Cords?" guide and many of the species profiles.

Our Editorial Process

Every article on iLoveTurtle goes through the same process:

  • Written by a subject-matter writer. Not outsourced to generic content farms.
  • Reviewed for factual accuracy. Health articles are reviewed by Dr. Chen.
  • Cited with sources. We link to journal articles, textbooks, and veterinary guidelines, not just blogs.
  • Updated over time. Each article shows a "last updated" date. When new research emerges, we revise.

What We Won't Do

  • We will not recommend a product just because a brand pays us.
  • We will not publish medical claims without a veterinary review.
  • We will not recycle content from other sites.
  • We will not pretend to know more than we do — when the science is uncertain, we say so.

Editorial Corrections

If you spot something on iLoveTurtle that looks wrong, outdated, or unsafe, please tell us. We take corrections seriously and update articles quickly when we are wrong. Reach out through our contact page.

Independence & Funding

iLoveTurtle is reader-supported. We may earn a commission if you buy through some affiliate links, but those links never influence which products or brands we recommend. Our editorial decisions are made independently of any commercial relationship.

Contact

For general enquiries, editorial corrections, or partnership questions, please visit our contact page or email us at hello@iloveturtle.com.
