We welcome feedback, corrections, and questions from readers. The fastest way to reach us is by email. Please use the address that matches your reason for contacting us so your message reaches the right person.

What to Include in Your Message

To help us respond quickly, please include:

  • For corrections: the page URL, the exact sentence or section that needs fixing, and a source or explanation if you have one.
  • For care questions: the species, age, and a description of the situation. We cannot diagnose or treat individual animals, but we can point you to relevant guides.
  • For partnerships: a short description of your company and what kind of collaboration you have in mind.

Response Times

We aim to respond to all messages within 3–5 business days. Correction reports are usually handled faster.

Medical Disclaimer

iLoveTurtle does not provide veterinary diagnosis or treatment. If your turtle is showing signs of serious illness — open-mouth breathing, refusing to eat, swelling, or shell damage — please contact a licensed exotic animal veterinarian as soon as possible. Our health guides are meant as supporting information, not a substitute for professional care.
